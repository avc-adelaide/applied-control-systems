\section{Concept 2.4.2: Stabilisation}

\subsection{Example 1: Stabilising an Unstable System via State Feedback}

\paragraph{Problem:} Consider an unstable system with dynamics:
\begin{align}
\frac{d}{dt}\begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -2 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}u, \quad y = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{bmatrix}
\end{align}

The system has one unstable eigenvalue at $\lambda_1 = 1$. Design a state feedback controller $u = -Kx$ to stabilise the system by placing the closed-loop eigenvalues at $\lambda_1 = -1$ and $\lambda_2 = -3$.

\paragraph{Solution:} 

For state feedback $u = -Kx$ where $K = \begin{bmatrix} k_1 & k_2 \end{bmatrix}$, the closed-loop system is:
\begin{align}
\frac{dx}{dt} = (A - BK)x
\end{align}

First, compute $BK$:
\begin{align}
BK = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} k_1 & k_2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} k_1 & k_2 \\ k_1 & k_2 \end{bmatrix}
\end{align}

The closed-loop matrix is:
\begin{align}
A - BK = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -2 \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} k_1 & k_2 \\ k_1 & k_2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1-k_1 & -k_2 \\ -k_1 & -2-k_2 \end{bmatrix}
\end{align}

The characteristic polynomial of the closed-loop system is:
\begin{align}
\det(sI - (A-BK)) &= \det\begin{bmatrix} s-(1-k_1) & k_2 \\ k_1 & s+2+k_2 \end{bmatrix} \\
&= (s-1+k_1)(s+2+k_2) - k_1k_2 \\
&= s^2 + (1+k_1+k_2)s + (2k_2 - 2 + k_1 + k_1k_2 - k_1k_2) \\
&= s^2 + (1+k_1+k_2)s + (k_1 + 2k_2 - 2)
\end{align}

The desired characteristic polynomial with eigenvalues $\lambda_1 = -1$ and $\lambda_2 = -3$ is:
\begin{align}
(s+1)(s+3) = s^2 + 4s + 3
\end{align}

Matching coefficients:
\begin{align}
1 + k_1 + k_2 &= 4 \\
k_1 + 2k_2 - 2 &= 3
\end{align}

From the first equation: $k_1 + k_2 = 3$

From the second equation: $k_1 + 2k_2 = 5$

Subtracting: $k_2 = 2$, therefore $k_1 = 1$

The state feedback gain is:
\begin{align}
K = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}
\end{align}

\textbf{Verification:} The closed-loop matrix is:
\begin{align}
A - BK = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -2 \\ -1 & -4 \end{bmatrix}
\end{align}

The characteristic polynomial is $\det(sI - (A-BK)) = s^2 + 4s + 3 = (s+1)(s+3)$ ✓

The system is now stable with eigenvalues at $-1$ and $-3$.
